## Limits of this validation

- B compares two numerical methods of the same model: it checks the integration, not the physics of the generic power curve.
- The generic power curve is smoother than a measured one (IEC 61400-12-1); real turbines need their own curve.
- NASA POWER is a coarse reanalysis (about 50 km): sites in complex terrain or on the coast can be far from reality.
- No comparison with measured production or with commercial software (WAsP, WindPRO).
- Results are preliminary-design quality, see DISCLAIMER.md.

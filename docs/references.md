# References

Every formula and typical value in RESize points to a publication, a standard or a dataset. Open the **Why?** panel of any step to see the sources of that step; the PDF report ends with the references it cites.

Standards and reports are revised: use the current edition when you cite them. Where a choice is RESize's own simplification and has no external source, the panel says so.

## Sources by topic

### Solar resource: in-plane irradiation H

- **Formula:** Duffie & Beckman (2013), ch. 1-2
- **Values and assumptions:** PVGIS (JRC), PVcalc tool; Huld et al. (2012), PVGIS database; Šúri et al. (2007), PVGIS method

### Performance ratio PR

- **Formula:** IEC 61724-1, definition of PR
- **Values and assumptions:** Reich et al. (2012), typical PR range; Jordan & Kurtz (2013), degradation, median about 0.5 %/year (not modelled)

### Required PV peak power

- **Formula:** Masters (2013), PV system sizing; Quaschning (2013)
- **Values and assumptions:** Reich et al. (2012), typical PR

### Temperature extremes

- **Formula:** IEC 62548, voltage at minimum temperature
- **Values and assumptions:** Skoplaki & Palyvos (2009), temperature coefficients

### Maximum modules per string

- **Formula:** IEC 62548; Masters (2013)
- **Values and assumptions:** Skoplaki & Palyvos (2009), temperature coefficients; Luque & Hegedus (2011), module behaviour with temperature

### Minimum modules per string

- **Formula:** Masters (2013)
- **Values and assumptions:** Faiman (2008), cell temperature above ambient; Skoplaki & Palyvos (2009)

### MPPT window at low temperature

- **Formula:** IEC 62548; Masters (2013)
- **Values and assumptions:** Skoplaki & Palyvos (2009), temperature coefficient of Vmp

### String layout (series and parallel)

- **Formula:** Masters (2013)
- **RESize's own choice:** All strings equal and equally oriented: a simplification of RESize.

### DC/AC ratio

- **Formula:** Masters (2013)
- **Values and assumptions:** Mondol et al. (2006), optimal DC/AC ratio

### Expected annual energy

- **Formula:** Masters (2013); Huld et al. (2012)
- **RESize's own choice:** Yearly energy balance only: use and export are not simulated.

### Shading by a nearby obstacle

- **Formula:** Quaschning & Hanitsch (1996)
- **Values and assumptions:** Duffie & Beckman (2013), sun position
- **RESize's own choice:** Flat obstacle band and a fixed diffuse share of 35 %: a RESize simplification.

### Production month by month

- **Formula:** Duffie & Beckman (2013)
- **Values and assumptions:** Huld et al. (2012), PVGIS monthly outputs
- **RESize's own choice:** The monthly shape is typical of the latitude, not a measured year.
